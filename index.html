Google Workspace support for owner-operated business workflows

ReadyKit Studio uses a private Google Workspace integration to help its owner manage business correspondence and scheduling. It is an internal operating tool—not a public Google sign-in, customer account, or signup service.

Contact ReadyKit Studio

The connection

Focused access for email and calendars

Gmail

Read access supports operator-requested correspondence workflows, and send access allows reviewed business replies and delivery. The app does not request permission to delete the full mailbox.

Google Calendar

Calendar and event access supports viewing calendars and managing scheduling. Calendar-list access is read-only, and the app does not control calendar sharing settings.

Google identity

Basic identity information identifies the owner's connected Google account. Arbitrary customer Google accounts cannot be connected through this site.

How it operates

Controlled by the business owner

Automation may assist with tasks the operator requests, such as organizing source-linked customer communications, preparing correspondence, coordinating schedules, and tracking delivery or replies. The operator reviews material external actions under the business workflow policy.

Google-derived data is used only to provide these requested functions. It is not sold, used for advertising, repurposed into marketing or prospect datasets, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

For details about access, storage, providers, retention, revocation, and deletion, read the Privacy Policy.
