Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

This policy explains how ReadyKit Studio handles information through its owner-controlled Google Workspace integration and this documentation site.

Who operates the app

ReadyKit Studio operates the app for its owner's business correspondence and scheduling. It is an internal tool connected to the owner's business Google account. It is not a service that lets customers or members of the public connect their own Google accounts.

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to hello.readykitstudio@gmail.com.

Google data and permissions

The app may access:

  • Gmail messages and related mailbox data needed to read business correspondence and send operator-authorized email;
  • Google Calendar calendars and events needed to view calendars and manage business scheduling; and
  • basic Google identity information needed to identify the connected owner account.

The Gmail permissions do not include full-mailbox deletion. Calendar-list access is read-only, and the app does not manage calendar sharing settings.

How information is used

Access is used only to provide operator-requested email and scheduling functions. Minimal local workflow records may include source-linked customer communications, scheduling information, contacts relevant to those tasks, and delivery or reply tracking. Automated processing may assist with requested business tasks, while material external actions are reviewed by the operator under the business workflow policy.

Personal Gmail content is not repurposed into marketing or prospect datasets. Google-derived data is not sold or used for advertising. It is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Limited use and disclosure

ReadyKit Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google and configured infrastructure or automation providers may process information where necessary to provide the requested features. Information is not shared for independent advertising, sale, or unrelated purposes. Human access is limited to the operator and authorized support when necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, or support the workflow.

Storage and protection

Credential storage uses OS-keyring encryption where supported and owner-only operating-system permissions for local workflow and server copies. Some credential and app storage is not encrypted. Access is limited to the systems and people needed to run or support the integration.

Tokens are never sent to or stored by these public documentation pages.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

App data is retained only as needed for the stated operation, subject to operational and legal obligations. The connected account's access can be revoked at any time through Google Account third-party connections.

Revoking access prevents future Google API access but does not automatically delete information previously stored by the app. To request deletion of retained app data, email hello.readykitstudio@gmail.com. ReadyKit Studio will address the request subject to applicable operational and legal obligations.

This documentation site

This static site has no app login, analytics, tracking scripts, or application cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard request logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the integration or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
